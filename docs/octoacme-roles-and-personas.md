# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Lead / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Product Leads or Executive Sponsors align the project with strategic goals, confirm business priority, and help resolve cross-functional trade-offs when delivery decisions affect scope, timeline, or resourcing.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic direction and approve project priorities or go/no-go decisions
- Help resolve competing needs across product, engineering, and business stakeholders
- Support major escalations, sponsorship, and risk-based decision making
- Confirm success metrics and business readiness for milestones or launch

### Goals
- Ensure the project remains aligned with business value and organizational priorities
- Remove blockers that require leadership attention
- Support clear decision-making and accountability at the executive level

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates, milestone reviews, and sponsor check-ins
- Business-impact and risk escalations
- Decision logs and executive summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers on product outcomes, priority-setting, and trade-offs
- Provides direction to Project Managers for timeline, staffing, and escalation paths
- Aligns with Developers and Engineering leads on feasibility and delivery risk when strategic trade-offs are required

---

## Engineering / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering or Tech Leads guide technical execution, architecture decisions, and delivery quality. They help the team turn requirements into a credible implementation plan and maintain technical consistency across the project.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach, architecture direction, and engineering standards
- Break down complex work into implementation plans and estimate delivery risk
- Support code quality, design reviews, and technical decision-making
- Identify dependencies, integration risks, and technical bottlenecks

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, reliable, and scalable implementation
- Reduce technical ambiguity and delivery risk
- Support the team in making sound engineering trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Architecture discussions and technical review sessions
- Dependency and risk updates during planning and execution
- Technical trade-offs and delivery forecasts with PMs and Product leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers on implementation quality, coding standards, and review expectations
- Coordinates with Project Managers on delivery estimates, dependency tracking, and risk reporting
- Works with Product Managers and Product Leads to assess feasibility, technical trade-offs, and priority impacts

---

## QA / Test Engineer

### Role Summary
QA and Test Engineers validate that work meets acceptance criteria, quality standards, and release readiness. They help ensure the team ships dependable features with clear evidence of quality.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, test cases, and acceptance validation approach
- Verify functionality, regression risk, and critical user flows
- Partner with Developers and Product teams on quality gates and defect triage
- Assess release readiness and communicate blockers or risks to stakeholders

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality expectations before release
- Reduce defects, regressions, and production risk
- Support confident decision-making on readiness and rollout

### Typical Communication
- QA signoff, test status, and release readiness updates
- Defect triage and quality risk communication
- Acceptance validation feedback for Product Managers and Project Managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers throughout implementation to define tests and validate fixes
- Collaborates with Product Managers and Product Leads on acceptance criteria and customer impact
- Keeps Project Managers informed of readiness, blockers, and testing dependencies

---

## UX/UI Designer or User Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers help the team understand user needs and shape experiences that are useful, accessible, and aligned with product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs, pain points, and feedback
- Design flows, prototypes, interaction patterns, and user-facing experiences
- Validate usability and accessibility with user testing and review
- Partner with Product and Engineering to refine solution fit and implementation trade-offs

### Goals
- Improve clarity, usability, and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework caused by unclear requirements or weak user experience
- Support product decisions with evidence and user-centered insights

### Typical Communication
- Research summaries, usability findings, and design reviews
- Product prioritization input and feedback loops with stakeholders
- Collaboration with QA on usability acceptance and edge cases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate customer needs into clear experience goals
- Advises Developers on implementation details, edge cases, and usability constraints
- Supports QA with user-acceptance scenarios and accessibility checks

---

## Operations / SRE or Release Manager

### Role Summary
Operations, SRE, or Release Managers help the team keep services reliable, observable, and ready for safe deployment and ongoing support.

### Responsibilities
- Define deployment readiness, release windows, and rollback considerations
- Monitor production health, reliability, and incident readiness
- Support observability, alerts, and post-release validation
- Coordinate with teams on incident communication and recovery actions

### Goals
- Minimize service disruption and production risk
- Improve deployment confidence and operational readiness
- Support a clear, repeatable release and recovery process

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews, rollback planning, and incident updates
- Monitoring and reliability status reports
- Coordination with Project Managers and Product Leads during launch and incident response

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA to verify deployment readiness and smoke-test coverage
- Collaborates with Project Managers and Product Leads on release timing, communication, and risk mitigation
- Supports Product teams by ensuring operational readiness and measurable post-release validation

---

## Role Combinations and Team Scaling

These roles are not always separate people. In smaller teams, one person may carry more than one function. For example:
- A small product team may combine Product Manager and Product Lead responsibilities
- A startup or focused project may combine Engineering Lead and Developer duties
- QA responsibilities may be shared among Developers during early-stage delivery
- A Release Manager may be assigned part-time for a single launch or milestone

The goal is to make ownership explicit. Every project should clarify who is accountable for product decisions, technical delivery, quality, risk, and deployment outcomes, even when responsibilities are combined.
